## Current direction

LambdaGen is moving from a collection of runtime experiments toward a coherent language and research platform. The next major engineering layer is the connection between language syntax, the algorithm registry, experiment runner, parallel execution, persistence and exported research tables.

## Research foundations

The design was compared against established mathematical and algorithmic references including NIST DADS, SageMath, PARI/GP and SymPy. NIST DADS provides a broad catalog of algorithms and data structures; SageMath organizes a large mathematical computing system by mathematical domains; PARI/GP provides extensive number-theory and computer-algebra functionality; and SymPy provides a large symbolic mathematics API.
